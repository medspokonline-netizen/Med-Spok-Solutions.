How we work

The equipment is the easy part

Anyone can put a concentrator in a box. What decides whether therapy actually happens is the fitting, the handover and the person who answers the phone at nine on a Sunday night.

We deliver to the room, assemble in place, verify oxygen purity and therapy pressure on site, fit the mask until the seal is right, and show whoever will be operating the equipment how to use it before we leave.
